About DailyGammon

What is DailyGammon?

A turn-based backgammon site. You sign up for matches and make your moves when you have time. Later, your opponent will log in and respond to your moves.

Top

How is DailyGammon different from most other backgammon web sites?

  • Matches go faster here thanks to computer guessing.
  • There are no advertisements.
  • Playing is free.

Who runs the site?

The Games

How are the dice rolls generated?

Random bytes are mixed with a local generator.

As an example:
byte# 1 2
value 17 203

Each byte becomes one roll.

Troubleshooting

What do I do if I forget my password?

 Send your user name to the help address. After you log in, you can change the password from your profile.
